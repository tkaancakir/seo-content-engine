# Choosing Supplies for Your First Home Craft Project

Start your first craft project with a small, specific goal\. Choose the supplies you need to complete that one project\.

Write down what the project requires before buying anything\. Check which tools and materials you already have\.

## How do you choose supplies for a first project?

Choose the tools and materials needed for the steps of one small project, then compare that list with what you already own\.

Decide what you want to make before collecting supplies\. You do not need to plan several projects at once\.

Compare your supply list with your existing tools\. Identify any missing items by walking through the project steps\.

Arrange your workspace so that materials are easy to reach\. Read the instructions for the tools and materials you plan to use\.

After your first attempt, note which supplies were useful and what you would change\. Use those observations to plan your next project\.

## Do I need to buy every supply at once?

Start by identifying what you need for a single project and what you already have\.

## Is a small desk enough?

The space you need depends on the project and the tools you use\. Check their setup and safety requirements\.

## How should I choose my first project?

Choose something that interests you and has a small number of steps you can follow\.
